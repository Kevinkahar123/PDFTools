Is PDFTools free?

Yes. PDFTools is designed to provide useful PDF and image utilities for free.

How do I merge PDF files?

Select two or more PDF files in Merge PDF and click Merge & Download PDF.

Can I convert PDF pages to images?

Yes. PDF to Image converts PDF pages into PNG images.

Can I create PDF from images?

Yes. Image to PDF supports JPG and PNG images.
